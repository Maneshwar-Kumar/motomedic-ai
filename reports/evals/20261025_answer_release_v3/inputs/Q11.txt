## SAFE RIDING TIPS / GUIDELINES (pages 8-11)
[E11] Before operating your new motorcycle, we request you to carefully read and follow the operating and maintenance instructions detailed in this manual for the safety of your own, your motorcycle and also that of others.
[E12] Know and adhere to the rules of the road with respect to your driving country.
[E13] Before starting the motorcycle, check for proper operation of brakes, clutch, gear shifter, handle bar controls, tyre pressures, fuel and oil levels, etc.
[E14] Use only genuine Royal Enfield spare parts and approved accessories. Use of other manufacturer's parts may affect the performance of your motorcycle and render the motorcycle void of warranty. Visit your Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre for details.
[E15] Whenever refueling your motorcycle, please exercise utmost caution and carefully observe the following guidelines.
[E16] Switch "OFF" mobile phones and other hand held electronic devices.
[E17] Do not smoke and please ensure that there are no open flames or sparks near the motorcycle, when refueling or servicing the fuel system.
[E18] Refuel in a well ventilated area with the engine turned off condition.
[E19] Open the fuel tank cap slowly.
[E20] Do not fill the fuel in tank to its brim. Please fill fuel till the bottom of anti splash plate, so as to leave sufficient air space in the fuel tank to allow for fuel expansion.
[E21] (WARNING) Royal Enfield cautions you against the use of certain nonstandard parts such as aftermarket and custom made extended front forks or suspensions, which may adversely affect performance and handling. Removing or altering original parts may adversely affect performance and could result in accident.
[E22] (WARNING) A new motorcycle must be operated according to the special running-in-procedure. See running-in-procedure mentioned in respective section.
[E23] (WARNING) Operate motorcycle only at moderate speeds and out of traffic until you have become thoroughly familiar with its operation and handling characteristics under all conditions.
[E24] (WARNING) Do not exceed the legal speed limit or ride too fast for existing conditions. Always reduce speed when poor riding conditions exist. High speed increases the influence of any other condition affecting stability and increases the possibility of loss of control.
[E25] (NOTE) If you are an inexperienced rider we recommend that you obtain formal training on correct motorcycle riding techniques and become thoroughly familiar with the operation of your motorcycle. New riders should gain experience under various conditions while driving at moderate speeds.
[E26] (NOTE) Pay strict attention to road surfaces and wind conditions. Any motorcycle may be subject to the following up-setting forces:
[E27] (NOTE) Wind blasts from passing vehicles.
[E28] (NOTE) Rough or uneven road surfaces.
[E29] Slippery road surfaces.
[E30] These forces may affect the handling characteristics of your motorcycle. If this happens, reduce speed of the motorcycle to a controlled condition. Do not apply brake abruptly.
[E31] Operate your motorcycle defensively. Remember that a motorcycle does not afford the same protection as an automobile in an accident. One of the most common accident situations occurs when the rider / driver of the other motorcycle / vehicle fails to see or recognise a motorcycle and turns into the oncoming motorcyclist.
[E32] Wear an approved helmet, clothing and footwear suited for riding a motorcycle. Bright / light colours are best for greater visibility in traffic, especially at night. Avoid loose, flowing garments and scarves.
[E33] When carrying a pillion rider, it is your responsibility instruct them on proper riding procedures.
[E34] 8
[E35] Do not allow other individuals, under any circumstances, to operate your motorcycle unless you know they are experienced, licensed riders and are thoroughly familiar with the operating conditions of your motorcycle.
[E36] (WARNING) Regularly inspect shock absorbers and front forks and look for leaks. Replace worn out parts. Worn out parts can adversely affect stability and handling.
[E37] (WARNING) Exhaust gas contains poisonous carbon monoxide and chemicals, known to cause cancer, birth defects or other reproductive defects, durability / longevity operation of your motorcycle.
[E38] (WARNING) For your personal welfare, all the listed service and maintenance recommendations should be performed. Lack of regular maintenance at the suggested intervals may affect the safe/durability/longevity operation of your motorcycle.
[E39] Avoid any contact with the exhaust system when hot. Wear clothing that will completely cover the legs while riding. The exhaust system gets very hot when the engine is running and remains too hot to touch, even after the engine is turned OFF. Failure to wear proper or protective clothing could result in serious injury.
[E40] Motorcycle batteries contain lead, acids and chemicals known to cause cancer, birth defects or other reproductive harm. Exercise extreme caution while handling a battery, wash hands thoroughly whenever a battery is handled.
[E41] Consult your Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre regarding any questions or problems that occur in the operation of your motorcycle. Failure to do so may aggravate an initial problem, cause costly repairs and jeopardize your personal safety.
[E42] Do not tow a motorcycle. The steering and handling of the towed motorcycle will be impaired due to the force of the towline. If a motorcycle must be transported, use a truck or a trailer.
[E43] Do not pull a trailer behind a motorcycle. Towing a trailer may cause reduced braking efficiency, tyre overloading and unstable handling, as it may cause loss of control of the motorcycle in the front, leading to an accident.
