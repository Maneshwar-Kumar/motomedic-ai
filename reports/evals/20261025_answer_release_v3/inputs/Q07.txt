## TROUBLESHOOTING (pages 121-122)
[E5] We have listed below a few basic checks in case your motorcycle is not functioning. If in case the problem is not rectified after these checks, it is necessary to get the motorcycle checked by a Royal Enfield Authorised Dealer/ Service Centre to rectify the problem and to ensure trouble free performance.
[E6] Symptom: Engine does not start; Observations: If the ignition/Engine kill switch in OFF position; Check for/Remedy: Switch ON ignition
    Observations: If inadequate fuel level in fuel tank; Check for/Remedy: Top up the fuel
    Observations: If the lights are dim/weak horn sound; Check for/Remedy: Weak or discharged battery / problem in charging circuit Contact Authorised service center
    Observations: If fuse is blown; Check for/Remedy: 1. Replace the fuse with same rating 2. Contact Authorised service center if prob- lem persists
    Observations: Connection issue with spark plug, cap, high tension cable; Check for/Remedy: Reconnect spark plug, cap and high tension cable.
[E7] Symptom: Engine starts but vehicle OFF's automatically when apply the first gear; Observations: Side stand indicator is glowing in the cluster; Check for/Remedy: Side stand should be kept up right
    Symptom: Engine misfires & runs erratically/stops.; Observations: If any adulteration/water in fuel; Check for/Remedy: Contact Authorised service center
    Observations: If the engine is too hot; Check for/Remedy: Switch OFF the engine and allow it to cool down
    Symptom: Poor pickup; Observations: If engine RPM raises disproportionately to the vehicle speed; Check for/Remedy: Adjust the clutch free play and contact Authorised service center
    Symptom: ABS (Anti - lock Braking System); Observations: If the ABS lamp continuously ON; Check for/Remedy: Contact Authorised service center

## PRECAUTIONS (pages 74-75)
[E10] Vehicle may start with side stand condition but engine will turn-off when gear is engaged, this side stand cutoff feature is provided for rider safety ensure side stand is retracted before vehicle moving / starting.
[E11] (CAUTION) If the engine does not start. Do not hold the starter switch in depressed condition for long periods, this will cause the battery to drain below the threshold level of 10 V. Please get the motorcycle checked through an Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre to identify and correct the reason for not starting.
[E12] Never accelerate as soon as the engine starts, especially in cold condition. The engine should be allowed to run in idle rpm for at least 120 seconds for the engine oil to circulate and lubricate all the internal moving parts and for the engine temperature to raise. Failure to adhere to this important information will cause serious damage to the engine internals.
[E13] Accelerate only after the idling rpm has stabilized and it is consistent.
[E14] If the acceleration cable is damaged, should be replace the entire RH side module cube.
[E15] (WARNING) Please exercise extreme care while riding the motorcycle. Failure to do so can result in an accident causing injury to you or to other road users / passerby.
[E16] (WARNING) Ensure gear in neutral position and the neutral lamp is glowing in the speedometer. To shift into neutral, move the motorcycle back and forth gently, while simultaneously shifting the gear.
[E17] (CAUTION) Do not attempt to shift gears without moving the motorcycle back and forth as it will damage the gears mechanism.
[E18] (CAUTION) Depress and hold the clutch Lever.
[E19] (CAUTION) Press starter button and hold till engine starts. Do not release the button before engine starts.
[E20] (CAUTION) Do not press the starter button more than 5 seconds after three successive cranking, wait for 15 to 20 seconds the battery to recover.
