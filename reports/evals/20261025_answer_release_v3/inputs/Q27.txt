## TAIL LAMP/TRAFFICATOR & LICENSE PLATE ILLUMINATOR (page 110)
[E1] The tail lamp/trafficator (B) & license plate illuminator (A) has a LED lighting system. In the event of failure, the entire tail lamp/trafficator (B) & license plate illuminator (A) assembly should be replaced.
[E2] Contact an Royal Enfield Authorised Dealer/Service Centre to replace the same.

## HEADLAMP DISMANTLING (pages 107-108)
[E3] Gently loosen and remove the head light beam adjustment screw.
[E4] Disconnect head lamp coupler.
[E5] Gently loosen and remove the LH & RH side head lamp holder Allen screws by using 4 mm Allen key.
[E6] (NOTE) The headlamp has a LED lighting system. In the event of failure, the headlamp LED assembly should be replaced.
[E7] (NOTE) Contact an Royal Enfield Authorised Dealer/Service Centre to replace the same.
