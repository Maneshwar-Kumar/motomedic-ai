RIDER QUESTION: Why will the ignition key not come out while it is ON?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## IGNITION KEY (page 26)
[E1] "OFF"
[E2] "ON"
[E3] (NOTE) Key can be removed only if ignition is in OFF or steering is locked.
[E4] (NOTE) Key is common for ignition, fuel tank lock, steering lock and pillion seat.
[E5] (NOTE) Key can be removed from fuel tank and pillion seat only in locked position from the key slots.
[E6] (WARNING) Do not switch OFF ignition while riding the motorcycle.
[E7] (WARNING) Doing so can cause a potential accident, resulting in serious injury to both rider and other road users, besides causing severe damage to the motorcycle.

## IGNITION/ENGINE KILL SWITCH (page 28)
[E8] "OFF"
[E9] "ON"
[E10] (CAUTION) Turn OFF ignition switch when engine is not running. Failure to do so will discharge the battery due to the headlamp being continuously ON.
