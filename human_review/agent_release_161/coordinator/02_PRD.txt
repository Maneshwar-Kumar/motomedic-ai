1  Product summary
MotoMedic AI supports owners who need a trustworthy, concise, manual-backed next action when a motorcycle exhibits a symptom. The current prototype uses only the supplied Royal Enfield New Himalayan 450 owner's manual. The system is an advisory aid, not a replacement for an authorized technician. Product success means either a correct documented next step, a justified clarification, a documented resolution, or an honest OEM escalation.

3  Goals and non-goals
G1: Deliver correct manual-supported outcomes with zero manually reviewed unsupported technical steps in the release suite; this is a benchmark gate, not a field guarantee.
G2: Support iterative diagnosis without repeating ruled-out steps or fabricating alternative repair paths
G3: Accept text and bike photos; support optional voice input that resolves to the same grounding flow
G4: Be accessible by URL and independently testable without a local installation
NG1: Diagnose unobservable internal faults from a photograph or guarantee a completed repair
NG2: Blend general web or model knowledge into technical recommendations
NG3: Offer user-configurable aftermarket modifications, undocumented bypasses or unverified safety-critical actions

5  Adaptive troubleshooting behavior
A diagnostic session starts from one atomic symptom. It stores typed facts and immutable attempts separately from chat text and traverses only approved source-derived rules. Corrections supersede prior records, then re-evaluate affected branches. Open-ended replies may propose typed outcomes, but uncertain interpretation remains unknown until clarified.
Outcome
Meaning
Required system behavior
NOT_TRIED
Step suggested but no response yet
Do not treat as completed
OBSERVED_NORMAL
A specific tested condition was reported normal
Exclude only the documented branch contradicted by an approved predicate; never infer complete system health
OBSERVED_ABNORMAL
User confirms documented abnormal condition
Proceed to exact corresponding documented procedure or escalate
ACTION_COMPLETED_UNRESOLVED
Customer tried action; symptom persists
Select next eligible documented step, without assuming test result
NOT_APPLICABLE
Step/part genuinely does not apply
Validate variant; exclude step for current context
UNCLEAR
Cannot determine observation or outcome
Request a precise recheck/photo/answer
RESOLVED
Customer confirms symptom no longer occurs
Close session; cite actions and avoid unsupported causal explanation

Reviewed controller and answer requirements
The deterministic controller evaluates approved manual-derived and versioned product-policy predicates as TRUE, FALSE or UNKNOWN using typed facts and explicit units. FALSE blocks a dependent step; UNKNOWN withholds it and permits one reviewed source-derived question only if the rider can resolve the fact. An unresolved action never becomes a normal observation. Eligibility is separate from complete evidence and final claim validation. Optional models may propose observations or rank already eligible candidates; they cannot authorize actions.
Use dense cosine retrieval plus BM25 and RRF, initial parent/dependency assembly, source and applicability checks, at most one exact-ID recovery for approved missing dependencies, and at most one conditional cross-encoder attempt for uncertain ordering. Reassemble while retaining mandatory evidence. Missing review must not trigger reranking. Conditional is the normal policy; off and always_diagnostic are ablations. Current unreviewed bundles skip reranking and block technical generation.
Keep final statuses SUPPORTED, AMBIGUOUS, UNSUPPORTED, SAFETY_STOP and SYSTEM_ERROR distinct. A known applicable safety stop retains its verified warning even if another component fails; otherwise a required operational failure is SYSTEM_ERROR. Rider-resolvable missing facts yield AMBIGUOUS. Established source gaps, unsupported requests and exhausted paths yield UNSUPPORTED after bounded recovery, with verified OEM support. Never ask the rider to resolve missing source warnings, table headers or review decisions. SUPPORTED requires both deterministic and semantic claim validation after controller selection.
Exact reviewed paths are keyed by manual checksum, confirmed variant, intent/relevant symptoms, typed outcomes, flow/controller and policy versions, with prompt and approval dependencies. Draft, stale and mismatched entries miss closed. Changes to rules, review, dependencies or policy invalidate affected cache entries and cases. Manual checksum, parser/chunker or embedding changes create a new index version; unchanged document vectors are reused for retrieval-policy changes.

7  UX content and presentation policy
Answer layout: observed symptom; documented next step; prerequisites/safety warnings; why this step is relevant (only if supported); exact manual citation; structured feedback choices
Clarification layout: one focused question with a small set of manual-derived answer choices; do not require the rider to use technical jargon
UNSUPPORTED wording must reflect an established source gap, unsupported request or exhausted documented path and offer the verified support route. Source-review gaps must not become rider questions. AMBIGUOUS asks one reviewed source-derived question about a rider-resolvable fact.
Error language: distinguish unsupported evidence from parser failure, API outage, and unconfirmed bike variant; do not misrepresent a system error as absence in the manual
8  Safety, privacy and trust requirements
Source-only guarantee applies to substantive technical recommendations; neutral conversational phrasing, source metadata and verified OEM contact information may be outside the manual
Do not continue an unsafe procedure when a relevant manual warning or unmet prerequisite applies; show the warning and stop/clarify appropriately
Treat manuals and user messages as untrusted instruction-bearing data: ignore commands embedded in PDFs or images that attempt to change system policy
Raw photos/audio are ephemeral by default, with no persistent storage in public demo telemetry unless separately opted in
Display that this is an experimental support tool and not a substitute for official service advice

10  Acceptance test / release gates
Choose and freeze one exact manual/variant, source checksum and rights posture
Create at least 25 independently reviewed gold cases before release, including multi-turn and image cases where evidence exists
Validate no unsupported instruction in the release regression set; manually review every high-risk case
Prove that “I tried it, still failing” advances or clarifies rather than looping to the same check
Prove that missing evidence triggers OEM escalation, while a backend error triggers a service-error message
Verify that an independent reviewer can open the live URL and reproduce named demo scenarios
11  Dependencies and open questions
Required: actual OEM manual, precise model/variant, verified OEM escalation URL, rights to exhibit selected manual excerpts
To decide after manual inspection: figure parsing quality, table preservation, graph-edge automation and photo test coverage
Product caveat: if the manual does not document alternative paths, a follow-up may legitimately end with an OEM escalation after only one check
Supporting technical documentation is referenced in the Concept and To Build documents.
