Executive concept
MotoMedic AI is a shareable assistant for an identified motorcycle model and a versioned Royal Enfield New Himalayan 450 owner's manual. Users may describe a symptom in text, upload a motorcycle photo, or optionally dictate a voice message. The assistant retrieves and composes only manual-supported advice, cites the relevant pages, and explicitly escalates to OEM support when evidence is unavailable.
Product thesis  The retrieval system locates evidence; a diagnostic state machine chooses the next eligible documented action; a fail-closed release gate prevents unsupported technical instructions from reaching the customer.

End-to-end logical architecture

Both build and runtime are hosted. Versioned ingestion parses, chunks and embeds the approved owner manual before rider requests; runtime reuses the immutable dense/BM25 index and reviewed source relationships. Retrieval assembles candidates, bounded recovery and conditional reranking resolve retrieval uncertainty, the controller selects an eligible documented step, and deterministic plus semantic validation gates the final response. Adaptive troubleshooting: what happens after feedback?
The assistant treats customer feedback as an observation, not as authorization to assume a diagnosis. “That component looked fine,” “I completed the action but the symptom remains,” and “that condition does not apply” are different structured outcomes.

Conceptual evidence policy
A technical instruction must cite the exact applicable evidence object (manual ID, section, printed/page index, optional image bounding box)
Attach prerequisites, cautions, warnings, variant-specific specifications and referenced sections before composing a procedural answer
When a reviewed rider observation is missing, ask one source-derived question. Missing source objects or review are maintainer issues and block advice; never complete a procedure from general motorcycle knowledge.
Use document-linked OEM service-routing metadata to provide an escalation path after all applicable documented options are exhausted
If retrieval infrastructure is unavailable, return “temporarily unable to verify,” never the false claim that the information is absent
Guardrails and release checks
Source and applicability gate: confirm the selected owner manual checksum, model and variant before any procedure; block advice when applicability is unresolved.
Evidence gate: assemble the relevant procedure, conditions, warnings, specifications, tables and linked pages; require every technical claim to map to an immutable source object and page.
Safety gate: surface source warnings and unmet prerequisites, stop unsafe or undocumented bypass requests, and refer to authorised service when the manual gives no safe next step.
Dialogue guardrail: ask one discriminating, source-derived question at a time; store typed outcomes; never infer that an attempted action was normal or repeat it without a documented reason.
Untrusted input guardrail: treat PDF text, images and user messages as data; ignore embedded instructions that try to change source, safety or citation policy.
Failure distinction: insufficient evidence triggers clarification or honest abstention; a parser, retrieval or model outage triggers a system-error message, never a false claim that the manual lacks information.
Cache guardrail: cache exact reviewed golden paths by manual checksum, variant, flow and policy version. Invalidate on any relevant change, and never use similarity alone to select a cached repair answer.
Privacy and cost guardrail: keep photos and audio ephemeral by default, redact traces, protect secrets, and apply rate and spending limits to a public demo.

Reviewed eligibility and response policy
The deterministic controller evaluates approved manual-derived and versioned product-policy predicates as TRUE, FALSE or UNKNOWN using typed facts and explicit units. FALSE blocks a dependent step; UNKNOWN withholds it and permits one reviewed source-derived question only if the rider can resolve the fact. An unresolved action never becomes a normal observation. Eligibility is separate from complete evidence and final claim validation. Optional models may propose observations or rank already eligible candidates; they cannot authorize actions.
Keep final statuses SUPPORTED, AMBIGUOUS, UNSUPPORTED, SAFETY_STOP and SYSTEM_ERROR distinct. A known applicable safety stop retains its verified warning even if another component fails; otherwise a required operational failure is SYSTEM_ERROR. Rider-resolvable missing facts yield AMBIGUOUS. Established source gaps, unsupported requests and exhausted paths yield UNSUPPORTED after bounded recovery, with verified OEM support. Never ask the rider to resolve missing source warnings, table headers or review decisions. SUPPORTED requires both deterministic and semantic claim validation after controller selection.
Exact reviewed paths are keyed by manual checksum, confirmed variant, intent/relevant symptoms, typed outcomes, flow/controller and policy versions, with prompt and approval dependencies. Draft, stale and mismatched entries miss closed. Changes to rules, review, dependencies or policy invalidate affected cache entries and cases. Manual checksum, parser/chunker or embedding changes create a new index version; unchanged document vectors are reused for retrieval-policy changes.
