Executive concept MotoMedic AI is a shareable assistant for an identified motorcycle model and a versioned Royal Enfield New Himalayan 450 owner's manual. Users may describe a symptom in text, upload a motorcycle photo, or optionally dictate a voice message. The assistant retrieves and composes only manual-supported advice, cites the relevant pages, and explicitly escalates to OEM support when evidence is unavailable. Product thesis  The retrieval system locates evidence; a diagnostic state machine chooses the next eligible documented action; a fail-closed release gate prevents unsupported technical instructions from reaching the customer. User promise and boundaries In scope Out of scope Guided, multi-turn troubleshooting with remembered results General mechanical knowledge, unsupported diagnosis, or repair improvisation Citations to exact OEM pages, including warnings and tables Claiming a visual observation proves a hidden root cause Text and photo input; optional speech-to-text Unrestricted speech-to-speech repair advice Honest refusal + verified OEM support route Saying “not in the manual” when the index or parser failed End-to-end logical architecture Both build and runtime are hosted. Versioned ingestion parses, chunks and embeds the approved owner manual before rider requests; runtime reuses the immutable dense/BM25 index and reviewed source relationships. Retrieval assembles candidates, bounded recovery and conditional reranking resolve retrieval uncertainty, the controller selects an eligible documented step, and deterministic plus semantic validation gates the final response. The diagrams show the target contracts; current progress is recorded below. Adaptive troubleshooting: what happens after feedback? The assistant treats customer feedback as an observation, not as authorization to assume a diagnosis. “That component looked fine,” “I completed the action but the symptom remains,” and “that condition does not apply” are different structured outcomes. Evidence gate: assemble the relevant procedure, conditions, warnings, specifications, tables and linked pages; require every technical claim to map to an immutable source object and page. Safety gate: surface source warnings and unmet prerequisites, stop unsafe or undocumented bypass requests, and refer to authorised service when the manual gives no safe next step. Dialogue guardrail: ask one discriminating, source-derived question at a time; store typed outcomes; never infer that an attempted action was normal or repeat it without a documented reason. Untrusted input guardrail: treat PDF text, images and user messages as data; ignore embedded instructions that try to change source, safety or citation policy. Failure distinction: insufficient evidence triggers clarification or honest abstention; a parser, retrieval or model outage triggers a system-error message, never a false claim that the manual lacks information. Cache guardrail: cache exact reviewed golden paths by manual checksum, variant, flow and policy version. Invalidate on any relevant change, and never use similarity alone to select a cached repair answer. Privacy and cost guardrail: keep photos and audio ephemeral by default, redact traces, protect secrets, and apply rate and spending limits to a public demo. Reviewed eligibility and response policy The deterministic controller evaluates approved manual-derived and versioned product-policy predicates as TRUE, FALSE or UNKNOWN using typed facts and explicit units. FALSE blocks a dependent step; UNKNOWN withholds it and permits one reviewed source-derived question only if the rider can resolve the fact. An unresolved action never becomes a normal observation. Eligibility is separate from complete evidence and final claim validation. Optional models may propose observations or rank already eligible candidates; they cannot authorize actions. Keep final statuses SUPPORTED, AMBIGUOUS, UNSUPPORTED, SAFETY_STOP and SYSTEM_ERROR distinct. A known applicable safety stop retains its verified warning even if another component fails; otherwise a required operational failure is SYSTEM_ERROR. Rider-resolvable missing facts yield AMBIGUOUS. embedding model's recommended metric. Sparse BM25 retrieval handles exact warning labels, component codes and units. Fuse ranks with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, assemble initial parent and verified dependency context, and inspect ranking uncertainty separately from source applicability and evidence coverage. Conditionally rerank a bounded candidate pool before reassembly and the controller/answer gates. The hosted prototype uses a fused pool of 20 candidates and selects five hits for assembly. One recovery may widen the fused pool to 40 and fetch exact reviewed dependency IDs. Reranking scores at most 20 candidates and 100 token-bounded query/passage pairs. At most 256 mandatory dependency IDs may be attached; an over-limit manifest fails as SYSTEM_ERROR and must not be silently truncated. These limits and trigger thresholds are versioned starting hypotheses, to be calibrated on independently reviewed cases. Apply hard filters by selected manual ID, model/variant, publication version and language before ranking Edge expansion should be selective: always include required warnings/prerequisites; retrieve only referenced figures/specifications, not the whole manual For each candidate, compute evidence coverage over requested step, conditions, warnings, variant-specific values and referenced sections If an approved dependency set is incomplete, permit one bounded recovery that fetches the exact required objects and widens retrieval once. If dependencies remain unavailable, withhold technical advice and record a non-rider-resolvable source gap. Allow at most one subsequent reranking attempt when ordering remains uncertain and all source/applicability checks permit it; never loop between search, reranking and generation. Cosine similarity and reranker scores are relevance heuristics, never proof that an instruction is supported 4 1 Evidence dependency manifest During versioned ingestion, bind each searchable child chunk to its full parent procedure or section and build a reviewed dependency manifest for every actionable step. The manifest identifies the action spans, applicability conditions, prerequisites, warnings, table headers and units, variant labels, and referenced figures or sections required to present that step. A section parent remains contextual evidence until its procedure and step boundaries have been reviewed. The assembler returns required_evidence_ids, attached_evidence_ids and missing_evidence_ids for the selected candidate, together with dependency_manifest_version and dependency_review_status. missing_evidence_ids is the required set minus the attached set. Coverage is complete only when the dependency manifest is approved for the exact manual checksum, the action evidence is present, all required objects are attached, and their content and provenance pass extraction checks. An empty or unreviewed required set cannot establish sufficiency. Finding a table object is insufficient if its required headers, units or variant context are missing. Perform the existing bounded recovery search for missing dependencies and record its outcome. A source gap that the rider cannot resolve must block the instruction and enter source review or abstention; do not ask the rider to supply a missing manual warning or table header. Retrieval outages, invalid index objects and other operational failures return SYSTEM_ERROR. Cross-encoder scores rank relevance and cannot approve a dependency manifest, infer a diagnostic order or waive missing evidence. 4 2 Conditional reranking and bounded recovery The normal request path uses conditional reranking. Clearly ordered candidates with complete reviewed dependencies continue to the eligibility and answer gates. Reranking handles uncertain ordering among plausible candidates; evidence recovery handles required objects absent from the initial bundle. Similarity, RRF and cross-encoder scores cannot prove support or approve a procedure. Trigger signals compare distinct parent sections: disagreement between dense and BM25 top sections, or a narrow relative RRF margin between the two leading sections. Sibling chunks from the same section do not create ambiguity. Zero BM25 matches provide no sparse-agreement signal. Initial experimental values are top-three section overlap below 0.5 or relative RRF margin at most 0.05. Mark these values PROVISIONAL_UNCALIBRATED; use reviewed tuning cases to calibrate them and held-out cases to assess their effect before a release decision. Apply the routing policy in order. A known safety stop retains its verified warning and skips model work; a known system failure remains SYSTEM_ERROR; a known unsupported request abstains. Missing, stale or unapproved source/dependency review enters source review or abstention. Unconfirmed applicability enters one focused clarification; a confirmed mismatch blocks the action. An approved missing dependency triggers one exact-ID fetch and bounded retrieval expansion. Complete eligible evidence with uncertain ordering triggers one cross-encoder attempt; otherwise continue through the remaining gates. Reassemble after reranking and recheck the dependency set. Retain all already fetched mandatory action, warning, prerequisite, table/header/unit and figure objects for the selected reviewed step even when candidate ordering changes. Reranking does not select a new diagnostic step or infer an OEM order. The controller remains responsible for selecting an eligible step and the final verifier remains responsible for claim support. Operational failures in embedding, retrieval, assembly or the cross-encoder yield SYSTEM_ERROR with a typed error class and recorded attempts. A query exceeding the current cross-encoder's 128-token input bound, or work exceeding 100 query/passage pairs, skips that inference with an explicit reason and keeps ranking unresolved. Preserve the full query and source text; never silently truncate a rider report or mandatory evidence. A source gap cannot be resolved by asking the rider to supply a missing manual warning or table header. Return a RetrievalDecision with policy version/configuration, calibration status, execution mode, uncertainty signals, decision history, recovery/rerank attempt counts, exact missing/fetched evidence IDs, pair count, skip reason and stage durations. Conditional mode is the normal code path; off and always_diagnostic modes exist only for ablations. Always-diagnostic execution can bypass the scheduling decision but cannot bypass evidence, applicability, safety or answer-release gates. A generic insufficient-bundle flag must never trigger reranking by itself. The current hosted manual has pending applicability and independent dependency review. Its candidate bundles therefore skip conditional reranking with explicit review/clarification reasons and keep may_generate_procedural_advice false. The implementation is available for approved registry/controller inputs; source and release approvals are separate work. Changing this policy versions retrieval and invalidates affected response caches while reusing unchanged document embeddings. Evaluate off, always and conditional policies on the same independently reviewed cases. Record complete evidence recall, rescued and regressed cases, proper abstention, wrong-variant leakage, trigger precision/recall, reranking frequency, per-stage latency, TTFO and cost per correctly handled case. Adopt calibrated conditional reranking for release only if it improves reviewed outcomes without violating latency/cost and safety gates. Cross-encoder retrieval reference: https://www.sbert.net/examples/sentence_transformer/applications/retrieve_rerank/README.html 5  Diagnostic state machine and adaptive branching A deterministic controller stores the diagnostic session separately from the LLM chat transcript. The controller accepts typed feedback and selects eligible actions only from the verified procedure graph. Generation occurs after next-step selection, never before. 5 1 Reviewed step registry and rule authoring Create candidate StepDefinition and ApplicabilityRule records from the original owner manual during ingestion. A layout parser or model may propose the records, but an independent reviewer must check each action, condition, prerequisite, warning and stopping condition against the original pages before it becomes runtime-authorized. Record the reviewer, decision, source spans and registry version. Draft, rejected, stale or checksum-mismatched rules must not authorize an action or a cached answer. Every manual-derived predicate must retain its supporting evidence IDs and page provenance. Record origin as MANUAL for OEM conditions and PRODUCT_POLICY for application behavior such as avoiding repeated attempts; policy rules carry their own version and must never be presented as OEM instructions. Relevance tags and rider intent mappings help select candidates, while the reviewed predicates establish applicability. Uncertain interpretation of the rider report remains an unknown observation until clarified. When the manual lists possible causes or independently documented checks without branch conditions or an explicit next-step order, preserve those limits. Do not infer a condition, eliminate an undocumented branch, diagnose a fault from a negative clue or claim an OEM ranking from retrieval scores. The existing SM-04 policy governs selecting among independently documented eligible checks. 5 2 Three valued eligibility checks Evaluate reviewed conditions against typed session facts using TRUE, FALSE and UNKNOWN. A condition is UNKNOWN when its observation is missing, uncertain or invalid; a confirmed mismatch is FALSE. A step is ineligible when any required condition is FALSE, its source or review status is invalid, its branch has been validly excluded, or its attempt history blocks repetition. If no required condition is false but a relevant condition is unknown, withhold the action and ask one reviewed source-derived question when the rider can reliably resolve it. Unknown is never treated as true or normal. An action becomes eligible only when the manual and variant match, the rule is approved, the symptom or documented branch applies, required conditions and prerequisites are true, no applicable safety rule prohibits it, and the attempt history allows it. Evaluate the evidence-completeness and answer-release gates separately before issuing technical text. A direct factual lookup uses the same applicability and evidence gates without fabricating a diagnostic step. Implement rule predicates with allowlisted typed comparisons such as enum equality, boolean facts and numeric comparisons with explicit units. Treat the PDF and model output as data; do not execute extracted expressions as code. Return an EligibilityDecision with each condition result, reason code, evidence reference, registry version and session version. ACTION_COMPLETED_UNRESOLVED records an attempted action and never supplies an OBSERVED_NORMAL fact. Optional interpretation models may propose typed observations or rank already eligible candidates; their output cannot bypass the controller gates. 5 3 Controller behavior Rule ID Controller behavior SM-01 Each suggested diagnostic step gets a stable step_id and an immutable attempt record; updates supersede earlier observations rather than delete them SM-02 Only structured observed outcomes can eliminate a branch; “tried but still failing” must not be converted into “normal” SM-03 Next-step eligibility uses approved StepDefinition and ApplicabilityRule records for the selected checksum/variant, typed condition results, satisfied prerequisites, no prohibiting safety condition and permitted attempt history. Relevance alone cannot authorize a step; complete evidence is required separately before release. SM-04 Prefer explicit OEM NEXT_STEP order; when no explicit order exists, only recommend another independently documented eligible check and do not imply OEM prioritization SM-05 When a reviewed branch condition is UNKNOWN, withhold the dependent action and ask one source-derived question if the rider can resolve it. If a source or extraction gap cannot be resolved by rider feedback, route to review/abstention; operational failures remain SYSTEM_ERROR. SM-06 Do not repeat attempted steps unless the customer corrects an observation, new symptoms change applicability, or the manual explicitly requires a repeat; explain why SM-07 If no eligible documented step remains, issue OEM escalation with a concise traceable recap SM-08 Model/version switch invalidates procedural branch assumptions and requires explicit reconfirmation SM-09 Safety warnings take precedence over ordinary branch selection; unsupported safe workarounds are forbidden SM-10 When a user reports resolution, close as customer-reported, not mechanically verified 6  Evidence sufficiency and output validation Apply two layers: a deterministic rules gate (valid manual, cited evidence IDs, required dependencies, prerequisites/variant, no explicit conflict) plus a semantic support evaluator that compares each proposed technical claim against relevant source spans. Reject the draft if either layer fails. 6 1 Response routing and release The controller selects the response route from structured eligibility decisions, the evidence bundle and gate results. A known applicable stop condition yields SAFETY_STOP and retains its verified warning even if other processing fails. Otherwise, a required component failure yields SYSTEM_ERROR. A resolvable missing applicability fact or documented branch observation yields AMBIGUOUS and one focused question. After bounded recovery, an unsupported request, an unresolved source gap or exhausted documented paths yields UNSUPPORTED with the verified support route. SUPPORTED requires an eligible action or applicable factual answer, an approved and complete dependency set, valid citations, satisfied safety and prerequisite checks, and successful final claim validation. The controller may authorize generation of a draft after next-step selection; the final response is marked SUPPORTED only after both validation layers pass. Rejected drafts remain withheld. Retry within a bounded policy, then return SYSTEM_ERROR for generation/verification failure or UNSUPPORTED for an established source gap; never disguise a system failure as absence in the manual.
