Rollback is a coordinated backend/UI operation. Retain both previous digest-addressed images and compatible Cloud Run revisions, their source/runtime manifest, and the session-schema/runtime-lock binding. Before any separately authorized traffic rollback, check that both prior revisions are available and reject current sessions whose locks cannot be read by that pair. Move both services to their recorded revisions and verify image/runtime/index identities and exact saved-response recovery. Do not change Qdrant, restart billing, activate fallback or open public access as a side effect. The image-binding tool refuses a missing rollback pair instead of guessing historical service revisions.
