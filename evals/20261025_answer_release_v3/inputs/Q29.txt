RIDER QUESTION: My bike has been stored for over a month. Can I just restart it?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## STORAGE PRECAUTIONS (page 120)
[E1] In-case your motorcycle is not going to be used for a month or more, the following precautions should be taken.
[E2] Get the motorcycle serviced through a Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre.
[E3] Drain the fuel completely from the fuel tank and induction system.
[E4] Clean drive chain thoroughly and apply Royal Enfield recommended chain lubricant.
[E5] Wipe off excess lubricant after 5 minutes of application.
[E6] Remove charging circuit fuse from the fuse box.
[E7] Store the battery in a cool, dry and well ventilated place.
[E8] Cover the silencer with suitable bags to prevent moisture entry. Set the motorcycle on its center stand.
[E9] Apply anti rust solutions on all plated parts. Take care not to apply this solution on chrome, rubber or painted parts. Store motorcycle in a clean covered area free of moisture and dust.
[E10] For re-use after storage, it is preferable to get the motorcycle prepared through a Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre to ensure the motorcycle is restored to its peak operating conditions.
[E11] If the motorcycle is not used for a month or longer, It is advised to disconnect battery terminals and remove the battery. Before refitting the battery in the motorcycle, check the battery voltage is within specification, if not, recharge it from authorized service workshop/ battery dealer.

## ROLL OVER SENSOR (page 68)
[E12] In the event of motorcycle falling over in either of its sides with the engine running and the gears engaged the roll over sensor will disable fuel systems and switch OFF the engine. This is to prevent any damage to the motorcycle and its rider.
[E13] To reset the roll over sensor and reactivate the fuel systems.
[E14] Ensure the motorcycle is made upright and is on its center stand.
[E15] Ensure gears are in correct neutral and the neutral lamp is glowing in the instrument console.
[E16] Switch OFF both ignition & stop switches, wait for a few seconds and switch ON the ignition and stop switch again, to start the engine.

## COOLANT DRAINING (pages 86-87)
[E17] Possibly remove the sump guard.
[E18] Remove the coolant filler cap on radiator and cap on expansion tank.
[E19] Remove the coolant drain bolt on the water pump.
[E20] Once drained, then replace the copper washer with a new part and tighten bolt to 10±2 Nm.
[E21] Expansion tank is drained by disconnecting small hose to radiator then refit the hose.
[E22] (WARNING) Never open a radiator cap when the engine is hot. The pressure released can make the coolant begin to boil and expand. Boiling coolant could spurt out of the filler neck or reservoir, causing severe burns.
[E23] (WARNING) Ensure vehicle is in straight position on ground level.
[E24] (WARNING) Remove the radiator pressure cap and fill the system with recommended coolant, up to the filler neck.
[E25] (WARNING) Carry out air bleeding.
[E26] Gently tap and squeeze both engine to radiator hoses.
[E27] Tip the bike to approximately 45-60 degree angle on the left and then the right side.
[E28] If coolant level has dropped, top back up to filler neck level.
[E29] Repeat above procedure until the level in the filler neck no longer drops.
[E30] Refit the radiator pressure cap ensuring it is correctly fitted & fully tightened clockwise.
[E31] Remove the radiator expansion tank cap and fill the tank to the upper level line.
[E32] Replace the radiator expansion tank cap.
[E33] Start the engine in neutral.
[E34] Run the engine at idle for 5 minutes.
[E35] Blip the throttle 3 or 4 times.
[E36] Stop the engine and allow it to cool.
[E37] Top up the coolant level in the expansion tank if required.
[E38] Check for coolant leaks.
[E39] (WARNING) Do not spill coolant. Collect coolant in a separate container and dispose it through authorized disposal agencies in your locality. Avoid skin or body contact with the coolant. Promptly wash affected area with soap and water.
[E40] (NOTE) Do not fill the coolant when a vehicle is on side stand condition.
[E41] (NOTE) Coolant from Total is ready to use and does not require mixing.

## PARKING MOTORCYCLE ON SIDE STAND (pages 79-80)
[E42] Park a motorcycle is upright on a firm and flat surface.
[E43] Extend side stand. Tilt the motorcycle to the left side, till it is supported firmly on the ground.
[E44] (WARNING) Ensure both stands are fully retracted before riding the motorcycle.
[E45] (WARNING) Please exercise extreme care while parking and ensure it is parked on a firm and flat surface to avoid the motorcycle from falling over and causing injury to you or to others and damage to the motorcycle parts.
[E46] (WARNING) The side stand is only designed for the weight of the motorcycle. Do not sit on the motorcycle when it is resting on the side stand. The side stand or frame may become damaged and the motorcycle may fall over.
