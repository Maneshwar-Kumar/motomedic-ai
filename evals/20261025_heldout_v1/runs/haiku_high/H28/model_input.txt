## INSPECTION OF TYRES AND WHEELS (pages 89-90)
[E1] Inspect the tyres periodically for tread wear, cracks and cuts.
[E2] Check and remove stone, splinters, nails or other particles embedded in the tyre treads.
[E3] Periodically inspect wheels for spokes damage and wheel rim for wobbling or run out.
[E4] Check proper seating of the tyre beading on the rim whenever the tyre is reassembled.
[E5] Use only recommended tyres, inflated to correct air pressure.
[E6] Tyre pressure: Solo & With Pillion; Front & Rear: 32 psi/2.24 kg/cm 2
[E7] (WARNING) It is recommended to operate the motorcycle with correct tyre pressure as under inflated tyre may cause tyre to overheat and may result in tyre damage and may cause injury to the rider.
[E8] (WARNING) It is recommended to use Royal Enfield tyre specification in case of replacement of new tyres, failure to
[E9] (WARNING) adhere the same may result in tyre damage and may cause injury to the rider.

## REAR SUSPENSION SETTING (pages 116-117)
[E10] Rest the motorcycle on center stand.
[E11] Ensure the tyre pressure is at recommended specification.
[E12] Ensure adjuster are clear of all dirt and debris before adjusting, if they have dirt or debris please clean it suitably before adjusting.
[E13] The adjuster is a 6 step adjuster and it is kept at first notch position.
[E14] Adjust shock preload by rotating the adjuster clockwise using C-spanner to increase the preload.
[E15] Adjust shock preload by rotating anti-clockwise using C-spanner to decrease preload (to make it to first notch position).
[E16] (NOTE) Adjusting the suspension requires a C-spanner. It is we recommend to use the C-spanner that is given in your vehicle tool kit. Extension tube to be used for leverage purposes.
[E17] (NOTE) The best spring preload setting is achieved when it is set for the weight of the rider and that of any luggage and a passenger.
[E18] We recommend to increase the preload and keep it at final notch for fully loaded (GVW condition), thus ensuring an ideal compromise between handling and stability.

## ACCESSORIES AND LUGGAGE (pages 14-15)
[E25] Royal Enfield offers a range of Genuine Motorcycle Accessories that have been fully approved and extensively tested alongside the motorcycle.
[E26] Therefore, the rider must be responsible for safe operation of the motorcycle when installing accessories or carrying additional weight.
[E27] Please adhere to the following guidelines when carrying a pillion, luggage or when fitting any accessories.
[E28] Do not exceed 110 km/h when riding solo, carrying a pillion or payload on an accessory equipped motorcycle.
[E29] Keep luggage weight concentrated close to the motorcycle and as low as possible; this minimizes sudden shift in the motorcycle's center of gravity.
[E30] Distribute weight evenly on both sides of the motorcycle.
[E31] Do not load bulky items behind the rider or add weight to the handlebars or front forks.
[E32] Re-check the luggage periodically to ensure it is secured and will not shift while riding. Accessories mounted loosely may affect the riding of the motorcycle and affect the handling and stability of the motorcycle.
[E33] Large surfaces such as fairings, windshields, backrests and luggage racks can adversely affect handling of the motorcycle. Use only Royal Enfield genuine motorcycle accessories which are model specific and follow installation procedure.
[E34] (WARNING) Do not load weight or install accessories incorrectly on the motorcycle. Doing so may affect the motorcycle's stability, handling characteristics and safe operation and could result in an accident causing serious injury or loss of life.
