## FRONT WHEEL REMOVAL (pages 91-93)
[E11] Ensure motorcycle is upright on a firm and flat surface.
[E12] Locate a scissor jack under the engine and lift motorcycle such that the front wheel is off the ground by minimum 15 cm.
[E13] Loosen and remove fork clamp bolt 2 Nos from LHS front fork assembly by using 6 mm Allen key available in the tool kit.
[E14] Loosen and remove spindle bolt from LHS front fork assembly by using front spindle adapter, ring spanner and extension tube are available in the tool kit.
[E15] Gently pull out the spindle from LHS by hand once fully unscrewed.
[E16] Gently lower the wheel such that it comes out of the fork legs.
[E17] Remove spacers from front wheel hub on both LHS and RHS.
[E18] (CAUTION) Take care to secure the wheel spacers and speed sensor while removing the axle from the forks.
[E19] Do not press the brake lever when front wheel is removed as this will result in the brake pads coming too far out of the brake caliper.
