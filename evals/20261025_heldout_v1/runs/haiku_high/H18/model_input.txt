RIDER QUESTION: When removing the battery, which terminal do I disconnect first, and which one goes back on first?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## DISMANTLING (pages 104-105)
[E1] Switch "OFF" the engine and remove ignition key from the key barrel.
[E2] The battery is located underneath the rider seat.
[E3] Remove the pillion and rider seat as per procedure.
[E4] Disconnect battery black negative (-ve) terminal bolt by using 10 mm spanner available in the tool kit.
[E5] Disconnect battery red positive (+ve) terminal bolt by using 10 mm spanner available in the tool kit.
[E6] Pull battery strap (belt) downwards and release strap lock from battery strap bracket.
[E7] Remove the battery from tray.
[E8] (WARNING) Always disconnect the black negative (-ve) battery cable first and then the red positive (+ve) cable while removing the battery connections.
[E9] (NOTE) Usage of frequency for motorcycle is very important for battery to be in good performance condition. If the motorcycle is being used very rarely or sparingly and the terminals are not disconnected the battery is bound to lose its charge and result in a dead battery.
[E10] (NOTE) For checking the battery voltage contact Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre or battery service centre.

## ASSEMBLY (pages 105-107)
[E11] Assemble battery into tray.
[E12] Pull battery strap (belt) downwards to fix strap lock into battery strap bracket.
[E13] Connect battery red positive (+ve) terminal bolt.
[E14] Connect battery black negative (-ve) terminal bolt.
[E15] Refit the rider and pillion seat as per procedure.
[E16] (CAUTION) Connect the red (+ve) positive terminal first and then connect black (-ve) negative terminal only.
[E17] (CAUTION) Keep the red (+ve) positive terminal and (-ve) negative terminal cables firmly connected to the respective battery terminals. Failure to do so may result in damage to the motorcycle electrical system.
[E18] (NOTE) Clean the wire terminals free from corrosion and keep the terminals coated with petroleum jelly.
