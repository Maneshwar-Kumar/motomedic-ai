## FRONT WHEEL ASSEMBLY (pages 93-95)
[E25] Remove the wooden piece/cardboard sheet placed between the brake pads.
[E26] Place a 4 mm thick wooden piece or cardboard sheet between the brake pads to avoid pads activation in the event the front brake lever is accidentally pressed.
[E27] Take care not to damage the front brake disc or toner ring as it will affect the braking system and ABS.
[E28] Locate spacer to the wheel hub on both LH and RH side.
[E29] Insert the wheel along with spacers between the fork ends. Ensure the brake disc is located between the brake pads.
[E30] Support the wheel at a height that the spindle can be fitted through the fork legs and wheel. Insert the spindle from the LHS and push until the threads of the spindle engage with those of the RHS fork leg then screw spindle until fully seated.
[E31] Tighten spindle bolt firmly on LH side.
[E32] Tighten fork clamp bolt firmly on LH side to a torque of 25 Nm.
[E33] Rotate wheel to check for smooth rotation.
[E34] Press brake lever and check front brake efficiency.
[E35] (CAUTION) Please exercise utmost caution while reassembling the front wheel on the motorcycle.
[E36] (CAUTION) Please ensure the wheel is fitted correctly before attempting to ride the motorcycle.
[E37] (CAUTION) Failure to do so may result in the motorcycle not performing correctly, may lead to an accident causing injury to you / other road users and may lead to loss of life.

## REAR SUSPENSION SETTING (pages 116-117)
[E38] Rest the motorcycle on center stand.
[E39] Ensure the tyre pressure is at recommended specification.
[E40] Ensure adjuster are clear of all dirt and debris before adjusting, if they have dirt or debris please clean it suitably before adjusting.
[E41] The adjuster is a 6 step adjuster and it is kept at first notch position.
[E42] Adjust shock preload by rotating the adjuster clockwise using C-spanner to increase the preload.
[E43] Adjust shock preload by rotating anti-clockwise using C-spanner to decrease preload (to make it to first notch position).
[E44] (NOTE) Adjusting the suspension requires a C-spanner. It is we recommend to use the C-spanner that is given in your vehicle tool kit. Extension tube to be used for leverage purposes.
[E45] (NOTE) The best spring preload setting is achieved when it is set for the weight of the rider and that of any luggage and a passenger.
[E46] We recommend to increase the preload and keep it at final notch for fully loaded (GVW condition), thus ensuring an ideal compromise between handling and stability.
