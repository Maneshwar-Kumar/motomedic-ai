MANUAL SECTIONS:

## For Digital Screen (page 38)
[E1] 12:00 | 22
[E2] (NOTE) Fuel consumption will only be shown when the vehicle is under operation, and the fuel range value will be updated while the vehicle is driven condition.

## IF PRESS JOYSTICK LEFT (pages 36-37)
[E4] The following details are displayed on the advanced dashboard.
[E5] Trip 1
[E6] Trip 2
[E7] Fuel range
[E8] Instantaneous fuel consumption
[E9] Battery gauge
[E10] Service Due In
[E11] Engine temperature
[E12] The above details can be customized in my vehicle option available in the settings menu.

## iii. Fuel consumption (pages 49-50)
[E13] Center press the joystick with the fuel consumption option selected to explore the options.
[E14] The following options are available in the menu.
[E15] km/l
[E16] L/100 km
[E17] mpg (UK)
[E18] mpg (US)
[E19] The options can be navigated by joystick up and down press and selected by joystick center press.
[E20] Press the home button on the LH switch cube to go back.
[E21] (NOTE) If the speed/distance is in km then either km/l or L/100 km options are available to select and the remaining options are grayed out.
[E22] 0
[E23] 0
[E24] If the speed/distance is not in km (in mpg) then either mpg (UK) or mpg (US) options are available to select and the remaining options are grayed out.
