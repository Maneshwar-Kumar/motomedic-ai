RIDER QUESTION: How do I check the brake fluid level, and what fluid should I top up with?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## CHECKING BRAKE FLUID (pages 88-89)
[E1] Front: Check that the brake fluid reservoir is horizontal and that the fluid level is center of the window consider as a minimum level.
[E2] Rear: Check that the brake fluid reservoir is horizontal and that the fluid level is between the "MAX" level and "Min" level marks.
[E3] Place your motorcycle in an upright position on a firm, level surface.
[E4] (CAUTION) Brake fluid is highly corrosive and can cause damage to painted parts. Please ensure that brake fluid does not spill on any part of the motorcycle. In the event of a spill, please clean the area immediately with a soft cloth (preferably a wet cloth) to avoid damage.
[E5] (CAUTION) Do not mix DOT 4 & other brake fluid together.
[E6] (NOTE) Clean the filler cap before removing. Use only DOT 4 brake fluid from sealer container.
[E7] (NOTE) Use only the specified brake fluid; otherwise, the rubber seals may deteriorate, causing leakage.
[E8] (NOTE) Refill with the same type of brake fluid. Adding a brake fluid other than DOT 4 may result in a harmful chemical reaction.
[E9] Be careful that water or dust does not enter the brake fluid reservoir when refilling. Water will significantly lower the boiling point of the fluid and may result in vapor lock and dirt may clog the ABS hydraulic unit valves.
[E10] (WARNING) An insufficient brake fluid level will cause the brake system to fail.
[E11] (WARNING) Old brake fluid reduces the braking effect.
[E12] (WARNING) Make sure that brake fluid for the front and rear brake is changed in accordance with the periodic maintenance schedule.
[E13] (WARNING) Keep brake fluid out of the reach of children.
[E14] (WARNING) Do not allow brake fluid to come into contact with the skin, the eyes or clothing.
[E15] (WARNING) Brake fluid is highly corrosive and can cause damage to painted parts. Ensure brake fluid does not spill on any part of the motorcycle, in the event of spill please clean the area immediately with a soft cloth to avoid damage.
