RIDER QUESTION: How do I switch the instrument cluster from the analog layout to the digital one?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## iv. Screen type (pages 42-43)
[E1] Center press the joystick with the screen type option selected to change the screen type of the instrument cluster.
[E2] There are two options available: Analog and digital screen.
[E3] Default the screen is in analog type.
[E4] The options can be navigated by joystick up and down press and selected by joystick center press.
[E5] Press the home button on the LH switch cube to go back.
[E6] Screen can be changed from analog to digital by long pressing the Mode button on the RH switch cube.
[E7] 0

## iii. Brightness (page 42)
[E8] Center press the joystick with the brightness option selected to change the brightness of the instrument cluster.
[E9] The brightness shall be increased by joystick right press and decreased by joystick left press.
[E10] Press the home button on the LH switch cube to go back.
[E11] 0
[E12] (NOTE) Brightness can be adjusted only in the dark theme.

## ii. Firmware version (page 53)
[E13] Center press the joystick with the firmware version option selected to view the current firmware version of the instrument cluster.
[E14] Press the home button on the LH switch cube to go back.
