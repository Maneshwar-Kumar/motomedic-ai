RIDER QUESTION: How do I take off the rider and pillion seats, and put them back?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## PILLION AND RIDER SEAT DISMANTLING (pages 34-35)
[E1] Insert the key on the pillion seat key slot and turn clockwise direction.
[E2] Slide the seat towards forward direction to remove the pillion seat.
[E3] Then slide the rider seat backward direction to remove the rider seat.
[E4] (NOTE) The rider seat height adjustment option is available in the bottom side of the rider seat, if increase or decrease in the rider seat height just change the front and rear end seat rod slot.
[E5] (NOTE) Place the removed seat in a safe location to prevent from scratches and dirt.

## PILLION AND RIDER SEAT ASSEMBLY (pages 35-36)
[E6] Slide the seat forward direction and ensure the rider seat aligned with frame.
[E7] Place the pillion seat and slide the seat towards backward direction.
[E8] Ensure the pillion seat aligned with frame and press seat to lock with key in position.

## AIR FILTER CLEANING (pages 113-114)
[E9] The air filter is located underneath fuel tank.
[E10] Remove the pillion and rider seats as per procedure.
[E11] Remove the fuel tank bracket Allen bolt 3 Nos by using 5 mm Allen key available in the tool kit.
[E12] Disconnect the fuel flow hose and fuel tank sensor.
[E13] Pull out the fuel tank outside from frame.
[E14] Loosen and remove the air filter cover Allen screw 3 Nos by using 4 mm Allen key available in the tool kit.
[E15] Pull out filter element and check for dust, dirt and oil particles inside air filter box clean carefully.
[E16] Take out the cover from air filter box.
[E17] (NOTE) Usage of high pressure compressed air is not recommended to clean air filter element. Fitment of air filter element is in the reverse order of removal process.

## ASSEMBLY (pages 105-107)
[E18] Assemble battery into tray.
[E19] Pull battery strap (belt) downwards to fix strap lock into battery strap bracket.
[E20] Connect battery red positive (+ve) terminal bolt.
[E21] Connect battery black negative (-ve) terminal bolt.
[E22] Refit the rider and pillion seat as per procedure.
[E23] (CAUTION) Connect the red (+ve) positive terminal first and then connect black (-ve) negative terminal only.
[E24] (CAUTION) Keep the red (+ve) positive terminal and (-ve) negative terminal cables firmly connected to the respective battery terminals. Failure to do so may result in damage to the motorcycle electrical system.
[E25] (NOTE) Clean the wire terminals free from corrosion and keep the terminals coated with petroleum jelly.
