## FUEL TANK CAP (page 27)
[E6] Lift key flap on fuel tank cap and insert key.
[E7] Turn key clockwise to open.
[E8] Press cap to lock with key in position.
[E9] Remove key from cap and close flap.
[E10] (WARNING) Do not overfill the fuel tank.
[E11] (WARNING) Fill fuel only till the bottom of anti splash plate.
[E12] (WARNING) Over filling may result in gasoline entering the EVAP canister and may damage the evaporative emission system.
[E13] (CAUTION) Gasoline vapour is highly explosive. Please ensure there are no open flames or sparks nearby while refueling and fill fuel only in a well ventilated area.
[E14] (CAUTION) Please ensure gasoline does not spill on painted surfaces. In case fuel spills over the painted surfaces wipe it off immediately as it may leave a permanent stain.
[E15] (CAUTION) Do not smoke while refueling or when fuel tank cap is open.

## Distance to empty (pages 38-39)
[E16] Distance to empty is a features which shows how much distance the vehicle can run while in reserve tank/low fuel indicator starts glowing.
[E17] This will start showing in the cluster when fuel gauge shows low fuel indicator.

## STARTING (pages 72-74)
[E18] To shift into neutral, move the motorcycle back and forth gently, while simultaneously shifting the gear. Ensure gear is in neutral position and the neutral lamp is glowing in the instrument cluster.
[E19] Turn ignition key to ON position and engine kill switch on right hand side handle bar to run position.
[E20] When both the ignition and engine kill switch is in "ON" position and after the vehicle is started, the MIL will glow for a few seconds and turns "OFF", this indicates
[E21] that all the function of the electronic fuel injection (EFI) system is functioning normally. In the event of any malfunction in the EFI system the MIL will glow continuously.
[E22] 5
[E23] (CAUTION) In case the malfunction indicator does not turn OFF, get the motorcycle checked through an Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre for rectification.
[E24] (CAUTION) Check the fuel level indicator in the cluster for adequate fuel in the fuel tank. In case the last bar is blinking continuously, it indicates low fuel level in the tank. Please re-fuel immediately.
[E25] (CAUTION) Disengage clutch by pulling in the clutch lever and hold it in depressed condition.
[E26] (CAUTION) Push and hold electric start switch until engine starts for a maximum of 5 seconds.
[E27] (NOTE) In case the engine does not start within 5 seconds, release starter switch and wait for about 5 seconds before attempting to start the engine again.
[E28] (NOTE) In case vehicle not starting on multiple continuous attempts, please turn OFF and turn ON ignition switch and then start again.
