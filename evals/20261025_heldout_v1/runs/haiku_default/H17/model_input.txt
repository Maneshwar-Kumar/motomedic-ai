## COOLANT DRAINING (pages 86-87)
[E1] Possibly remove the sump guard.
[E2] Remove the coolant filler cap on radiator and cap on expansion tank.
[E3] Remove the coolant drain bolt on the water pump.
[E4] Once drained, then replace the copper washer with a new part and tighten bolt to 10±2 Nm.
[E5] Expansion tank is drained by disconnecting small hose to radiator then refit the hose.
[E6] (WARNING) Never open a radiator cap when the engine is hot. The pressure released can make the coolant begin to boil and expand. Boiling coolant could spurt out of the filler neck or reservoir, causing severe burns.
[E7] (WARNING) Ensure vehicle is in straight position on ground level.
[E8] (WARNING) Remove the radiator pressure cap and fill the system with recommended coolant, up to the filler neck.
[E9] (WARNING) Carry out air bleeding.
[E10] Gently tap and squeeze both engine to radiator hoses.
[E11] Tip the bike to approximately 45-60 degree angle on the left and then the right side.
[E12] If coolant level has dropped, top back up to filler neck level.
[E13] Repeat above procedure until the level in the filler neck no longer drops.
[E14] Refit the radiator pressure cap ensuring it is correctly fitted & fully tightened clockwise.
[E15] Remove the radiator expansion tank cap and fill the tank to the upper level line.
[E16] Replace the radiator expansion tank cap.
[E17] Start the engine in neutral.
[E18] Run the engine at idle for 5 minutes.
[E19] Blip the throttle 3 or 4 times.
[E20] Stop the engine and allow it to cool.
[E21] Top up the coolant level in the expansion tank if required.
[E22] Check for coolant leaks.
[E23] (WARNING) Do not spill coolant. Collect coolant in a separate container and dispose it through authorized disposal agencies in your locality. Avoid skin or body contact with the coolant. Promptly wash affected area with soap and water.
[E24] (NOTE) Do not fill the coolant when a vehicle is on side stand condition.
[E25] (NOTE) Coolant from Total is ready to use and does not require mixing.
