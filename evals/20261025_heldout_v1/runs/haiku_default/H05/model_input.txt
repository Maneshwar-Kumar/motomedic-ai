RIDER QUESTION: If I switch the hazard lights on and then turn the ignition off, how long will they keep flashing?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## Methods to activate hazard flasher: (pages 29-30)
[E1] Ignition switch ON, press the hazard switch. Hazard will work unless manual deactivation (OFF).
[E2] Ignition switch ON, press the hazard switch hazard will start working - Ignition switch OFF
[E3] Hazard will keep working for a duration of 30 minutes - Unless manual deactivation (OFF).
[E4] Hazard flasher cannot be activated in ignition switch OFF condition.
[E5] (WARNING) Turn signals do not work when the hazard light switch is "ON".
[E6] (WARNING) All the trafficator lamps will flash simultaneously.

## HAZARD LIGHT SWITCH (page 29)
[E7] Press the hazard button to hazard blinking ON.
[E8] (CAUTION) Hazard flashing will create a strain on battery. Do not use hazard warning for longer period unless necessary.

## IGNITION/ENGINE KILL SWITCH (page 28)
[E9] "OFF"
[E10] "ON"
[E11] (CAUTION) Turn OFF ignition switch when engine is not running. Failure to do so will discharge the battery due to the headlamp being continuously ON.

## GEAR POSITION INDICATION (pages 61-62)
[E12] Displays the gear position in which the vehicle is traveling.
[E13] When in neutral the indication will be "N".
[E14] When in gear the appropriate number between 1 to 6 will be displayed.
[E15] (1) Mobile connectivity symbol - It's shows mobile connecting and disconnecting indication.
[E16] (2) Ride mode - It can be viewed both performance mode and ECO mode indication based on the vehicle riding condition.
[E17] (3) ABS - Will be continuously ON during initial check up (until or after vehicle running for a particular distance / speed) and will turn OFF if system is ok, then will light up again in case of any ABS system malfunction.
[E18] (4) Turn right - Right turn signal ON.
[E19] (5) High beam indication - Head lamp high beam ON.
[E20] (6) Warning symbol - This indicator glows whenever the vehicle components fault.
[E21] (7) Turn left - Left turn signal ON.
[E22] (8) Engine MIL - Will be continuously ON in case of EMS malfunction.
[E23] (NOTE) Tell tales lights will switch "OFF" after engine is started.
[E24] (CAUTION) Do not run motorcycle in case the malfunction remains "ON" continuously as it can cause severe damage to the ECU & Sensors. Please visit the nearest authorised Royal Enfield dealer to diagnose & rectify the defect in the EMS.
[E25] (CAUTION) Do not run motorcycle in case the ABS indicator lamp continuously "ON".
