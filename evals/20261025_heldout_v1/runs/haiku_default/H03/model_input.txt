RIDER QUESTION: What engine oil grade should I use, and how much oil goes in for an oil change with a new filter?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## RECOMMENDED LUBRICANTS (page 21)
[E1] ENGINE OIL: Grade; 10W40 API SN, JASO MA2, Semi Synthetic; COOLANT: TOTAL COOLELF AUTO SUPRA -37 ºC; FRONT FORK OIL: SS-47G; BRAKE FLUID: DOT 4*
    ENGINE OIL: Capacity; 1 st dry fill: 2.4 L; COOLANT: Radiator: Dry fill - 915 ml Service fill - 815 ml; FRONT FORK OIL: RH fork: 589 ± 2.5 ml; BRAKE FLUID: Front - 90 ± 5 ml
    Refill: 2.1 L for oil service (Including filter change); COOLANT: Expansion Tank: Fill to max mark - 200 ml; FRONT FORK OIL: LH fork: 507 ± 2.5 ml; BRAKE FLUID: Rear - 80 ± 5 ml
[E2] (NOTE) Coolant from is ready to use and does not require mixing.
[E3] (NOTE) Recommendation subject to change without notice.
[E4] (NOTE) The above values are approximate and the actual capacity will vary.
[E5] (CAUTION) Use of wrong oil grade will reduce the life of the moving parts and seriously affect performance.

## OIL FILTER REPLACEMENT (page 85)
[E6] Remove the oil filter cover with O-ring and spring.
[E7] Remove and discard the oil filter.
[E8] Allow the oil to drain from the oil filter housing.
[E9] Thoroughly clean the cover and the O-ring sealing surface.
[E10] Install a new oil filter. Use the O-ring and lubricate the O-ring in the filter cover with water based lubricant.
[E11] Install the cover and tighten to 22±2 Nm, ensuring spring is retained in the cover.

## ENGINE OIL LEVEL CHECKING (page 83)
[E12] Place the vehicle on a level surface upright.
[E13] Start the engine from cold condition, allow the engine to idle for 2 to 3 minutes and stop the engine.
[E14] Wait 2 to 3 minutes to settle the oil and assess the sight glass. The volume added the minimum to maximum level is approximately 350 ml.
[E15] Engine oil level should be close to "MAX" condition.
[E16] If in case of oil level is not in above mentioned condition, then top-up the oil to "MAX" condition and repeat the procedure.
[E17] Do not over fill above the "MAX" mark as it may affect the clutch function.

## ENGINE OIL DRAINING/STRAINER CHECK (page 84)
[E18] Ensure vehicle is in straight position on ground level.
[E19] Engine oil should always be drained when the engine is warmed up sufficiently so that the oil drains faster.
[E20] Remove the sump guard and 2 plugs, then clean the 2 strainers.
[E21] Check the condition of strainers, if strainers condition OK please reuse it.
[E22] (NOTE) Do not reuse the O-rings, renew all O-rings.
[E23] (NOTE) Lubricate strainer and O-rings with engine oil.
[E24] (NOTE) Push strainers fully into crankcases by hand before inserting the drain plugs.
[E25] (NOTE) Lubricate plug O-rings with water based lubricant.
[E26] (NOTE) Tighten torque of drain plugs to 13±1 Nm.
