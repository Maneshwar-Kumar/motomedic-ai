RIDER QUESTION: How do I reset Trip 1 on the dashboard?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## i. Reset trip (page 44)
[E1] Center press the joystick with the reset trip option selected to reset the trip.
[E2] There are two options available: Reset trip 1 and reset trip 2.
[E3] 0
[E4] 0
[E5] The options can be navigated by joystick up and down press and selected by joystick center press.
[E6] Selecting either reset trip 1 or reset trip 2 will show a confirmation popup for reset.
[E7] Press the home button on the LH switch cube to go back.

## IF PRESS JOYSTICK LEFT (pages 36-37)
[E8] The following details are displayed on the advanced dashboard.
[E9] Trip 1
[E10] Trip 2
[E11] Fuel range
[E12] Instantaneous fuel consumption
[E13] Battery gauge
[E14] Service Due In
[E15] Engine temperature
[E16] The above details can be customized in my vehicle option available in the settings menu.
