## PRECAUTIONS (pages 74-75)
[E9] Vehicle may start with side stand condition but engine will turn-off when gear is engaged, this side stand cutoff feature is provided for rider safety ensure side stand is retracted before vehicle moving / starting.
[E10] (CAUTION) If the engine does not start. Do not hold the starter switch in depressed condition for long periods, this will cause the battery to drain below the threshold level of 10 V. Please get the motorcycle checked through an Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre to identify and correct the reason for not starting.
[E11] Never accelerate as soon as the engine starts, especially in cold condition. The engine should be allowed to run in idle rpm for at least 120 seconds for the engine oil to circulate and lubricate all the internal moving parts and for the engine temperature to raise. Failure to adhere to this important information will cause serious damage to the engine internals.
[E12] Accelerate only after the idling rpm has stabilized and it is consistent.
[E13] If the acceleration cable is damaged, should be replace the entire RH side module cube.
[E14] (WARNING) Please exercise extreme care while riding the motorcycle. Failure to do so can result in an accident causing injury to you or to other road users / passerby.
[E15] (WARNING) Ensure gear in neutral position and the neutral lamp is glowing in the speedometer. To shift into neutral, move the motorcycle back and forth gently, while simultaneously shifting the gear.
[E16] (CAUTION) Do not attempt to shift gears without moving the motorcycle back and forth as it will damage the gears mechanism.
[E17] (CAUTION) Depress and hold the clutch Lever.
[E18] (CAUTION) Press starter button and hold till engine starts. Do not release the button before engine starts.
[E19] (CAUTION) Do not press the starter button more than 5 seconds after three successive cranking, wait for 15 to 20 seconds the battery to recover.

## STARTING (pages 72-74)
[E20] To shift into neutral, move the motorcycle back and forth gently, while simultaneously shifting the gear. Ensure gear is in neutral position and the neutral lamp is glowing in the instrument cluster.
[E21] Turn ignition key to ON position and engine kill switch on right hand side handle bar to run position.
[E22] When both the ignition and engine kill switch is in "ON" position and after the vehicle is started, the MIL will glow for a few seconds and turns "OFF", this indicates
[E23] that all the function of the electronic fuel injection (EFI) system is functioning normally. In the event of any malfunction in the EFI system the MIL will glow continuously.
[E24] 5
[E25] (CAUTION) In case the malfunction indicator does not turn OFF, get the motorcycle checked through an Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre for rectification.
[E26] (CAUTION) Check the fuel level indicator in the cluster for adequate fuel in the fuel tank. In case the last bar is blinking continuously, it indicates low fuel level in the tank. Please re-fuel immediately.
[E27] (CAUTION) Disengage clutch by pulling in the clutch lever and hold it in depressed condition.
[E28] (CAUTION) Push and hold electric start switch until engine starts for a maximum of 5 seconds.
[E29] (NOTE) In case the engine does not start within 5 seconds, release starter switch and wait for about 5 seconds before attempting to start the engine again.
[E30] (NOTE) In case vehicle not starting on multiple continuous attempts, please turn OFF and turn ON ignition switch and then start again.

## REAR WHEEL REMOVAL (pages 95-98)
[E31] Ensure motorcycle is upright on a firm and flat surface.
[E32] Apply the center stand.
[E33] Observe and mark the alignment indexes on both chain adjuster on left and right side swing arm.
[E34] Loosen the lock nuts and adjuster nuts fully on the left and right side chain adjuster by using 24 mm ring spanner available in the tool kit.
[E35] Hold wheel spindle on left side firmly and loosen hex nut on right side.
[E36] Remove the nut and washer from the wheel spindle.
[E37] Push rear wheel fully into the swing arm.
[E38] Support rear wheel from bottom and pull out wheel spindle from the left side swing arm.
[E39] Release the brake hose gently and remove caliper assembly from swing arm on right side.
[E40] (CAUTION) Ensure brake hose does not get damaged or kinked while removing. Support caliper assembly suitably and away from swing arm.
[E41] (CAUTION) Release the chain from the sprocket and ensure it does not get jammed or damaged when removing rear wheel.
[E42] (CAUTION) Remove support from wheel bottom and gently slide out rear wheel from the swing arm with rear sprocket, brake disc and spacers.
[E43] (CAUTION) Do not press the rear brake pedal when the rear wheel is being removed as this will cause the brake pads to dislocate from the brake caliper.
[E44] (CAUTION) Place a 4 mm thick wooden piece or cardboard sheet between the brake pads to avoid activation of brake pads if rear brake pedal is accidentally pressed.
[E45] (CAUTION) Do not pull up the rear brake pedal to lift or raise the motorcycle for any reason.
