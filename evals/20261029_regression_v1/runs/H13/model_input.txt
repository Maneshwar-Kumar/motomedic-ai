RIDER QUESTION: My bike tipped over with the engine running and it cut out. How do I reset it so it starts again?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## ROLL OVER SENSOR (page 68)
[E1] In the event of motorcycle falling over in either of its sides with the engine running and the gears engaged the roll over sensor will disable fuel systems and switch OFF the engine. This is to prevent any damage to the motorcycle and its rider.
[E2] To reset the roll over sensor and reactivate the fuel systems.
[E3] Ensure the motorcycle is made upright and is on its center stand.
[E4] Ensure gears are in correct neutral and the neutral lamp is glowing in the instrument console.
[E5] Switch OFF both ignition & stop switches, wait for a few seconds and switch ON the ignition and stop switch again, to start the engine.

## COOLANT DRAINING (pages 86-87)
[E6] Possibly remove the sump guard.
[E7] Remove the coolant filler cap on radiator and cap on expansion tank.
[E8] Remove the coolant drain bolt on the water pump.
[E9] Once drained, then replace the copper washer with a new part and tighten bolt to 10±2 Nm.
[E10] Expansion tank is drained by disconnecting small hose to radiator then refit the hose.
[E11] (WARNING) Never open a radiator cap when the engine is hot. The pressure released can make the coolant begin to boil and expand. Boiling coolant could spurt out of the filler neck or reservoir, causing severe burns.
[E12] (WARNING) Ensure vehicle is in straight position on ground level.
[E13] (WARNING) Remove the radiator pressure cap and fill the system with recommended coolant, up to the filler neck.
[E14] (WARNING) Carry out air bleeding.
[E15] Gently tap and squeeze both engine to radiator hoses.
[E16] Tip the bike to approximately 45-60 degree angle on the left and then the right side.
[E17] If coolant level has dropped, top back up to filler neck level.
[E18] Repeat above procedure until the level in the filler neck no longer drops.
[E19] Refit the radiator pressure cap ensuring it is correctly fitted & fully tightened clockwise.
[E20] Remove the radiator expansion tank cap and fill the tank to the upper level line.
[E21] Replace the radiator expansion tank cap.
[E22] Start the engine in neutral.
[E23] Run the engine at idle for 5 minutes.
[E24] Blip the throttle 3 or 4 times.
[E25] Stop the engine and allow it to cool.
[E26] Top up the coolant level in the expansion tank if required.
[E27] Check for coolant leaks.
[E28] (WARNING) Do not spill coolant. Collect coolant in a separate container and dispose it through authorized disposal agencies in your locality. Avoid skin or body contact with the coolant. Promptly wash affected area with soap and water.
[E29] (NOTE) Do not fill the coolant when a vehicle is on side stand condition.
[E30] (NOTE) Coolant from Total is ready to use and does not require mixing.

## STARTING (pages 72-74)
[E31] To shift into neutral, move the motorcycle back and forth gently, while simultaneously shifting the gear. Ensure gear is in neutral position and the neutral lamp is glowing in the instrument cluster.
[E32] Turn ignition key to ON position and engine kill switch on right hand side handle bar to run position.
[E33] When both the ignition and engine kill switch is in "ON" position and after the vehicle is started, the MIL will glow for a few seconds and turns "OFF", this indicates
[E34] that all the function of the electronic fuel injection (EFI) system is functioning normally. In the event of any malfunction in the EFI system the MIL will glow continuously.
[E35] 5
[E36] (CAUTION) In case the malfunction indicator does not turn OFF, get the motorcycle checked through an Royal Enfield Authorised Service Centre for rectification.
[E37] (CAUTION) Check the fuel level indicator in the cluster for adequate fuel in the fuel tank. In case the last bar is blinking continuously, it indicates low fuel level in the tank. Please re-fuel immediately.
[E38] (CAUTION) Disengage clutch by pulling in the clutch lever and hold it in depressed condition.
[E39] (CAUTION) Push and hold electric start switch until engine starts for a maximum of 5 seconds.
[E40] (NOTE) In case the engine does not start within 5 seconds, release starter switch and wait for about 5 seconds before attempting to start the engine again.
[E41] (NOTE) In case vehicle not starting on multiple continuous attempts, please turn OFF and turn ON ignition switch and then start again.
