## CHARGER PORT (pages 64-65)
[E11] Charger port is located on the center part of handle bar.
[E12] (WARNING) Recommended not to use in rainy conditions to avoid damages to smart phones and charger, RE shall not be liable for any damage to smart phones.
[E13] (WARNING) Do not use any other device other than mobile phones, only one mobile shall be charged at one point of time.
[E14] (WARNING) Ensure proper insertion of USB cable, damages due to hard / wrong insertion of cable will not be entertained in warranty.
[E15] (WARNING) Do not leave the USB port cap opened / closed partially to avoid any short circuit when not in use.
[E16] (WARNING) Do not insert any metal or conductive materials inside USB charging port which may lead to short circuit.
[E17] (NOTE) Charging port is provided only for charging purpose and no data transfer is enabled.
[E18] (NOTE) Only constant charging mode is provided, fast / dash charging mode is not provided to be compatible with multiple makes mobile phones and cannot be compared with the performance of original smartphone chargers.
[E19] (NOTE) Duration of charging can be higher and will vary for different make mobile phones depending on smart phone battery capacity, SOC and smart phone charging circuits.
[E20] (CAUTION) Customer to ensure USB port cap is closed and locked properly when not in use, warranty will not be provided if port cap is damaged and not sealed properly.
[E21] (CAUTION) If high current draw greater than 2.5 A is observed USB charger will shutdown please check your smart phone battery current ratings before using.
[E22] (CAUTION) USB charger will function only when engine is in running condition.
[E23] (CAUTION) Use standard approved and high quality USB cables for proper functioning.
