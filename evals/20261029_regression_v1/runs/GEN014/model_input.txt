    Refill: 2.1 L for oil service (Including filter change); COOLANT: Expansion Tank: Fill to max mark - 200 ml; FRONT FORK OIL: LH fork: 507 ± 2.5 ml; BRAKE FLUID: Rear - 80 ± 5 ml
[E2] (NOTE) Coolant from is ready to use and does not require mixing.
[E3] (NOTE) Recommendation subject to change without notice.
[E4] (NOTE) The above values are approximate and the actual capacity will vary.
[E5] (CAUTION) Use of wrong oil grade will reduce the life of the moving parts and seriously affect performance.

## OIL FILLING DURING OIL SERVICE (pages 84-85)
[E6] Ensure vehicle is in straight position on ground level.
[E7] Remove the filler cap both cap and O-ring.
[E8] Add oil to the engine, fill until the oil level reaches the maximum mark on the sight glass.
[E9] Install the filler cap. Start the engine from cold condition and run for 1-2 minutes.
[E10] Switch off the engine and wait 1 minute.
[E11] Check that the oil level is between the minimum and maximum lines on the sight glass.
[E12] If not, top up the oil level so that it is between the minimum and maximum lines on the sight glass
[E13] Refill the specified fresh engine oil quantity 2.1 L.

## i. Clock (pages 47-48)
[E34] Center press the joystick with the clock option selected to explore the options.
[E35] The following options are available in the clock menu.
[E36] 24H
[E37] 12H
[E38] Set time
[E39] 0
[E40] 0
[E41] The options can be navigated by joystick up and down press and selected by joystick center press.
[E42] Press the home button on the LH switch cube to go back.
[E43] (NOTE) If the mobile phone is connected to the vehicle only the hour format (i.e. 24H & 12H) can be selected based on the rider preference.
[E44] (NOTE) If the mobile phone is not connected to the vehicle all the options (i.e. 24H, 12H & Set Time) are selected.
[E45] (NOTE) In the set time option, time can be adjusted by joystick up, down, left & right press.
[E46] (NOTE) Joystick up & down press is used to increase or decrease the hour, minute & time format values, while joystick left & right is used to switch between hour, minute & time format selection.
