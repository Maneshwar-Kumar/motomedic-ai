## PILLION AND RIDER SEAT ASSEMBLY (pages 35-36)
[E6] Slide the seat forward direction and ensure the rider seat aligned with frame.

## PILLION AND RIDER SEAT DISMANTLING (pages 34-35)
[E9] Insert the key on the pillion seat key slot and turn clockwise direction.
[E10] Slide the seat towards forward direction to remove the pillion seat.
[E11] Then slide the rider seat backward direction to remove the rider seat.
[E12] (NOTE) The rider seat height adjustment option is available in the bottom side of the rider seat, if increase or decrease in the rider seat height just change the front and rear end seat rod slot.
[E13] (NOTE) Place the removed seat in a safe location to prevent from scratches and dirt.
