RIDER QUESTION: Which direction can the headlamp beam be adjusted?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## HEAD LIGHT BEAM ADJUSTMENT (page 109)
[E1] The headlight beam focus can be adjusted in vertical direction by using the adjusting screw by turning clockwise or anti clockwise direction.
[E2] The adjusting screw is used to increase or decrease the height of the headlight beam in direction vertical direction only. This may be required to increase the visibility and to help prevent the other riders/vehicles passing nearby.

## HEADLAMP ASSEMBLY (pages 108-109)
[E3] Tighten and refit the LH & RH side head lamp holder Allen screws.
[E4] Connect the head lamp coupler.
[E5] Tighten and refit the head light beam adjustment screw.

## HEADLAMP DISMANTLING (pages 107-108)
[E6] Gently loosen and remove the head light beam adjustment screw.
[E7] Disconnect head lamp coupler.
[E8] Gently loosen and remove the LH & RH side head lamp holder Allen screws by using 4 mm Allen key.
[E9] (NOTE) The headlamp has a LED lighting system. In the event of failure, the headlamp LED assembly should be replaced.
[E10] (NOTE) Contact an Royal Enfield Authorised Dealer/Service Centre to replace the same.

## HIGH BEAM/LOW BEAM SWITCH (page 30)
[E11] When the headlamp is in "ON" condition High / Low beam will be selected by toggling the switch. High beam indicator tell tale located in instrument cluster will glow when high beam is selected.
[E12] High beam
[E13] Low beam

## iii. Brightness (page 42)
[E14] Center press the joystick with the brightness option selected to change the brightness of the instrument cluster.
[E15] The brightness shall be increased by joystick right press and decreased by joystick left press.
[E16] Press the home button on the LH switch cube to go back.
[E17] 0
[E18] (NOTE) Brightness can be adjusted only in the dark theme.
