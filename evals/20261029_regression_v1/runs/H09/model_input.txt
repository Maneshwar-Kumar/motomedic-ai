## ACCESSORIES AND LUGGAGE (pages 14-15)
[E13] Royal Enfield offers a range of Genuine Motorcycle Accessories that have been fully approved and extensively tested alongside the motorcycle.
[E14] Therefore, the rider must be responsible for safe operation of the motorcycle when installing accessories or carrying additional weight.
[E15] Please adhere to the following guidelines when carrying a pillion, luggage or when fitting any accessories.
[E16] Do not exceed 110 km/h when riding solo, carrying a pillion or payload on an accessory equipped motorcycle.
[E17] Keep luggage weight concentrated close to the motorcycle and as low as possible; this minimizes sudden shift in the motorcycle's center of gravity.
[E18] Distribute weight evenly on both sides of the motorcycle.
[E19] Do not load bulky items behind the rider or add weight to the handlebars or front forks.
[E20] Re-check the luggage periodically to ensure it is secured and will not shift while riding. Accessories mounted loosely may affect the riding of the motorcycle and affect the handling and stability of the motorcycle.
[E21] Large surfaces such as fairings, windshields, backrests and luggage racks can adversely affect handling of the motorcycle. Use only Royal Enfield genuine motorcycle accessories which are model specific and follow installation procedure.
[E22] (WARNING) Do not load weight or install accessories incorrectly on the motorcycle. Doing so may affect the motorcycle's stability, handling characteristics and safe operation and could result in an accident causing serious injury or loss of life.
[E23] (WARNING) Royal Enfield offers a range of Genuine Motorcycle Accessories that have been fully approved and extensively tested alongside the motorcycle.

## PARKING MOTORCYCLE ON SIDE STAND (pages 79-80)
[E27] Park a motorcycle is upright on a firm and flat surface.
[E28] Extend side stand. Tilt the motorcycle to the left side, till it is supported firmly on the ground.
[E29] (WARNING) Ensure both stands are fully retracted before riding the motorcycle.
[E30] (WARNING) Please exercise extreme care while parking and ensure it is parked on a firm and flat surface to avoid the motorcycle from falling over and causing injury to you or to others and damage to the motorcycle parts.
[E31] (WARNING) The side stand is only designed for the weight of the motorcycle. Do not sit on the motorcycle when it is resting on the side stand. The side stand or frame may become damaged and the motorcycle may fall over.
