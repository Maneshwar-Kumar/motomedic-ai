RIDER QUESTION: How full should I fill the fuel tank when refuelling?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## FUEL TANK CAP (page 27)
[E1] Lift key flap on fuel tank cap and insert key.
[E2] Turn key clockwise to open.
[E3] Press cap to lock with key in position.
[E4] Remove key from cap and close flap.
[E5] (WARNING) Do not overfill the fuel tank.
[E6] (WARNING) Fill fuel only till the bottom of anti splash plate.
[E7] (WARNING) Over filling may result in gasoline entering the EVAP canister and may damage the evaporative emission system.
[E8] (CAUTION) Gasoline vapour is highly explosive. Please ensure there are no open flames or sparks nearby while refueling and fill fuel only in a well ventilated area.
[E9] (CAUTION) Please ensure gasoline does not spill on painted surfaces. In case fuel spills over the painted surfaces wipe it off immediately as it may leave a permanent stain.
[E10] (CAUTION) Do not smoke while refueling or when fuel tank cap is open.

## RECOMMENDED LUBRICANTS (page 21)
[E11] ENGINE OIL: Grade; 10W40 API SN, JASO MA2, Semi Synthetic; COOLANT: TOTAL COOLELF AUTO SUPRA -37 ºC; FRONT FORK OIL: SS-47G; BRAKE FLUID: DOT 4*
    ENGINE OIL: Capacity; 1 st dry fill: 2.4 L; COOLANT: Radiator: Dry fill - 915 ml Service fill - 815 ml; FRONT FORK OIL: RH fork: 589 ± 2.5 ml; BRAKE FLUID: Front - 90 ± 5 ml
    Refill: 2.1 L for oil service (Including filter change); COOLANT: Expansion Tank: Fill to max mark - 200 ml; FRONT FORK OIL: LH fork: 507 ± 2.5 ml; BRAKE FLUID: Rear - 80 ± 5 ml
[E12] (NOTE) Coolant from is ready to use and does not require mixing.
[E13] (NOTE) Recommendation subject to change without notice.
[E14] (NOTE) The above values are approximate and the actual capacity will vary.
[E15] (CAUTION) Use of wrong oil grade will reduce the life of the moving parts and seriously affect performance.

## COOLANT DRAINING (pages 86-87)
[E16] Possibly remove the sump guard.
[E17] Remove the coolant filler cap on radiator and cap on expansion tank.
[E18] Remove the coolant drain bolt on the water pump.
[E19] Once drained, then replace the copper washer with a new part and tighten bolt to 10±2 Nm.
[E20] Expansion tank is drained by disconnecting small hose to radiator then refit the hose.
[E21] (WARNING) Never open a radiator cap when the engine is hot. The pressure released can make the coolant begin to boil and expand. Boiling coolant could spurt out of the filler neck or reservoir, causing severe burns.
[E22] (WARNING) Ensure vehicle is in straight position on ground level.
[E23] (WARNING) Remove the radiator pressure cap and fill the system with recommended coolant, up to the filler neck.
[E24] (WARNING) Carry out air bleeding.
[E25] Gently tap and squeeze both engine to radiator hoses.
[E26] Tip the bike to approximately 45-60 degree angle on the left and then the right side.
[E27] If coolant level has dropped, top back up to filler neck level.
[E28] Repeat above procedure until the level in the filler neck no longer drops.
[E29] Refit the radiator pressure cap ensuring it is correctly fitted & fully tightened clockwise.
[E30] Remove the radiator expansion tank cap and fill the tank to the upper level line.
[E31] Replace the radiator expansion tank cap.
[E32] Start the engine in neutral.
[E33] Run the engine at idle for 5 minutes.
[E34] Blip the throttle 3 or 4 times.
[E35] Stop the engine and allow it to cool.
[E36] Top up the coolant level in the expansion tank if required.
[E37] Check for coolant leaks.
[E38] (WARNING) Do not spill coolant. Collect coolant in a separate container and dispose it through authorized disposal agencies in your locality. Avoid skin or body contact with the coolant. Promptly wash affected area with soap and water.
[E39] (NOTE) Do not fill the coolant when a vehicle is on side stand condition.
[E40] (NOTE) Coolant from Total is ready to use and does not require mixing.

## Distance to empty (pages 38-39)
[E41] Distance to empty is a features which shows how much distance the vehicle can run while in reserve tank/low fuel indicator starts glowing.
[E42] This will start showing in the cluster when fuel gauge shows low fuel indicator.
