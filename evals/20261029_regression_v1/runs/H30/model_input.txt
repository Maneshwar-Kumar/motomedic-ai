7. If the drive chain free play is found to be incorrect adjust

(a) Loosen the rear wheel spindle.
(b) Push the rear wheel to the extreme front in the swing
arm slot.
(c) Turn the adjusting Allen bolt in small increments
evenly on both sides, until the specified slackness is
achieved. Wheel position in the slot on both sides
should always be kept equal between left and right.
(d) Tighten the wheel spindle torque value is 70 Nm and
tighten the adjusting Allen bolt torque value is 10 Nm.
Recheck the slackness in the chain to confirm it, if it’s
still not within specification, repeat the adjustment
procedure first three steps.
WARNING

chain slippage and may also cause increased wear rates
to chain and sprockets.
- Please ensure the both wheels are aligned correctly,
after adjusting the chain and before tightening the rear
wheel spindle nut.

## REAR WHEEL REMOVAL (pages 95-98)
[E15] Ensure motorcycle is upright on a firm and flat surface.
[E16] Apply the center stand.
[E17] Observe and mark the alignment indexes on both chain adjuster on left and right side swing arm.
[E18] Loosen the lock nuts and adjuster nuts fully on the left and right side chain adjuster by using 24 mm ring spanner available in the tool kit.
[E19] Hold wheel spindle on left side firmly and loosen hex nut on right side.
[E20] Remove the nut and washer from the wheel spindle.
[E21] Push rear wheel fully into the swing arm.
[E22] Support rear wheel from bottom and pull out wheel spindle from the left side swing arm.
[E23] Release the brake hose gently and remove caliper assembly from swing arm on right side.
[E24] (CAUTION) Ensure brake hose does not get damaged or kinked while removing. Support caliper assembly suitably and away from swing arm.
[E25] (CAUTION) Release the chain from the sprocket and ensure it does not get jammed or damaged when removing rear wheel.
[E26] (CAUTION) Remove support from wheel bottom and gently slide out rear wheel from the swing arm with rear sprocket, brake disc and spacers.
[E27] (CAUTION) Do not press the rear brake pedal when the rear wheel is being removed as this will cause the brake pads to dislocate from the brake caliper.
[E28] (CAUTION) Place a 4 mm thick wooden piece or cardboard sheet between the brake pads to avoid activation of brake pads if rear brake pedal is accidentally pressed.
[E29] (CAUTION) Do not pull up the rear brake pedal to lift or raise the motorcycle for any reason.

## REAR WHEEL ASSEMBLY (pages 98-100)
[E30] Ensure stepped spacer is located on the brake disc side, the lip of the spacer should be inserted through the bearing seal.
[E31] Ensure spacer is located along the sprocket side on the wheel hub.
[E32] Ensure that the chain adjuster are located properly inside the swing arm left and right sides.
[E33] Locate caliper assembly on the tab along the swing arm right side.
[E34] Locate rear wheel with the sprocket to the left side ensuring the brake disc in between the brake pads on right side.
[E35] Lift up the rear wheel and ensure that the slots in the swing arm brake caliper bracket holes in chain adjusters and the center hole in the hub are aligned.
[E36] Support rear wheel suitably and insert rear wheel spindle along the left side swing arm into the wheel hub.
[E37] Tab spindle gently into wheel hub slot till the threads are completely visible on the right side.
[E38] Assemble the drive chain on the sprocket and ensure it is seated correctly.
[E39] Check for free and smooth rotation of the rear wheel.
[E40] Assemble washer and hex nut on wheel spindle on right side.
[E41] (NOTE) Do not tighten hex nut fully.
[E42] (NOTE) Tighten chain adjuster nuts on left and right adjuster such that the index marks are aligned correctly on both sides of the swing arm.
[E43] (NOTE) Check and ensure correct chain tension and wheel alignment.
[E44] (NOTE) Hold spindle firmly on left side and tighten hex nut on right side.
[E45] (NOTE) Locate the caliper assembly on swing arm right side.
[E46] (NOTE) Check rear brake for proper operating efficiency.
[E47] (CAUTION) Please exercise utmost caution while reassembling the rear wheel on the motorcycle.
