## CLUTCH CABLE FREE PLAY INSPECTION / ADJUSTMENT (pages 101-102)
[E1] Clutch cable free play, plays a major role in clutch life & it is recommended to adjust whenever required for good clutch life.
[E2] Ensure the cable fitted correctly.
[E3] Steering handlebar to right hand lock position.
[E4] Using a 12 mm spanner, loosen the adjusting nut closest to the lifter arm fully and measuring the instrument.
[E5] Adjust the position of the inner adjusting nut to provide 9 - 11 mm of free play in the clutch lever ball end (from its furthest position) before engine engagement.
[E6] Pull the lever 3 times and check the gap again, adjust as per the previous point procedure if necessary.
[E7] Using both spanners, tighten the nut closest to the lifter arm without moving the inner nut.
[E8] Rotate the handlebar 5 or more times, returning to a straight position and re-check the free play in the lever.
[E9] 9.Adjust as per steps 4 & 5 procedure if necessary.
[E10] (CAUTION) If you are not comfortable to adjust free play as per stated procedure, please visit near by service centre.
[E11] (CAUTION) If desired free play is not achieved or there is a suspect of clutch slip- keep positive free play & reach nearest service centre.
[E12] (CAUTION) Adjuster nut should rest properly in the threaded region.
[E13] (CAUTION) Clutch free play should be checked and adjusted only when the engine is cold.
[E14] (CAUTION) During clutch play checking, check the clutch cable for any abnormality as it is in vehicle condition.
[E15] (CAUTION) If any abnormality suspected, reach nearest service centre.

## GENERAL CLUTCH LEVER FREE PLAY (page 101)
[E16] Clutch lever free play should be set to ensure that at any position when moving from left hand (LH) to right hand (RH) there is a certain amount of free play present at all conditions.
