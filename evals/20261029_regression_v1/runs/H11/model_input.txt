RIDER QUESTION: How do I check the engine oil level correctly?

EARLIER CONVERSATION: none

MANUAL SECTIONS:

## ENGINE OIL LEVEL CHECKING (page 83)
[E1] Place the vehicle on a level surface upright.
[E2] Start the engine from cold condition, allow the engine to idle for 2 to 3 minutes and stop the engine.
[E3] Wait 2 to 3 minutes to settle the oil and assess the sight glass. The volume added the minimum to maximum level is approximately 350 ml.
[E4] Engine oil level should be close to "MAX" condition.
[E5] If in case of oil level is not in above mentioned condition, then top-up the oil to "MAX" condition and repeat the procedure.
[E6] Do not over fill above the "MAX" mark as it may affect the clutch function.

## ENGINE OIL DRAINING/STRAINER CHECK (page 84)
[E7] Ensure vehicle is in straight position on ground level.
[E8] Engine oil should always be drained when the engine is warmed up sufficiently so that the oil drains faster.
[E9] Remove the sump guard and 2 plugs, then clean the 2 strainers.
[E10] Check the condition of strainers, if strainers condition OK please reuse it.
[E11] (NOTE) Do not reuse the O-rings, renew all O-rings.
[E12] (NOTE) Lubricate strainer and O-rings with engine oil.
[E13] (NOTE) Push strainers fully into crankcases by hand before inserting the drain plugs.
[E14] (NOTE) Lubricate plug O-rings with water based lubricant.
[E15] (NOTE) Tighten torque of drain plugs to 13±1 Nm.

## OIL FILLING DURING OIL SERVICE (pages 84-85)
[E16] Ensure vehicle is in straight position on ground level.
[E17] Remove the filler cap both cap and O-ring.
[E18] Add oil to the engine, fill until the oil level reaches the maximum mark on the sight glass.
[E19] Install the filler cap. Start the engine from cold condition and run for 1-2 minutes.
[E20] Switch off the engine and wait 1 minute.
[E21] Check that the oil level is between the minimum and maximum lines on the sight glass.
[E22] If not, top up the oil level so that it is between the minimum and maximum lines on the sight glass
[E23] Refill the specified fresh engine oil quantity 2.1 L.
